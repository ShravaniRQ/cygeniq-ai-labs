# Cygeniq AI Labs: HR Data Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the data retention schedule for all HR-related employee records to ensure compliance with privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

## 2. Retention Periods
- **Applicant Data (Unsuccessful):** Resumes and interview notes will be retained for 6 months after the position is filled.
- **Active Employee Records:** All active employee records (payroll, performance reviews) are retained for the duration of employment.
- **Terminated Employee Records:** Basic employment history and tax records are retained for 7 years post-termination.
- **Disciplinary Records:** Retained for 3 years, unless legal action is pending.

## 3. Data Destruction
At the end of the retention period, all digital records must be securely deleted from the database. Physical records must be shredded using approved third-party vendors.
